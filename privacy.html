Privacy

Last updated 6 September 2026

Dead Talks is an archive of channelled conversations. Reading it needs no account and collects nothing about you. An account is only needed to keep what speaks to you.

Reading without an account

You can browse every card, every quote and every passage, and listen to the episodes and audio journeys, without signing in. We do not run analytics, advertising or tracking scripts on this site, and we do not set cookies for visitors who are not signed in.

What we store if you make an account

  • Your email address and password. The password is stored only as a cryptographic hash, which cannot be read back.
  • Your display name — set from the part of your email before the @ when you sign up, and changeable at any time.
  • A profile picture, if you upload one.
  • A short "about" line, if you write one.
  • The quotes and cards you like. Liking something also saves it to your own list.
  • Your reflections — notes you write under a quote.

What other people can see

This part matters, so it is stated plainly.

  • Your display name and profile picture are public. So is anything you write in your "about" line.
  • Your likes are public. Every quote and card shows how many people have liked it, and anyone can tap that number to see who. Your name and picture appear in that list.
  • Your reflections are private. They are visible only to you. Nobody else — including us — reads them through the site.
  • Your email address is never shown to other people.

If you would rather not be seen liking something, do not like it — a like is a public gesture here, in the way it is on any social site.

Signing in with Google

If you choose "Continue with Google", Google tells us your email address, your name and your profile picture, and nothing else. We never see your Google password. You can disconnect Dead Talks at any time from your Google account permissions page.

Who else handles your data

  • Supabase stores the database, the accounts and the uploaded pictures.
  • Resend sends the confirmation and password-reset emails, and therefore handles your email address.
  • GitHub Pages serves this website.
  • Podbean serves the podcast audio when you press play, so a play is counted in the show's own statistics.
  • Spotify and Apple Podcasts are linked to, and their own terms apply once you follow a link.

We do not sell your data, and we do not share it with anyone beyond the services above, which are used only to run the site.

How long we keep it

For as long as your account exists. Ask us to delete your account and everything listed here goes with it — profile, likes, saved list and reflections.

Your choices

  • Change your name, picture or about line at any time from your profile.
  • Unlike anything, and it leaves both the public count and your list.
  • Delete any reflection you have written.
  • Ask for your account and everything in it to be deleted.

Contact

Write to hello@thedeadtalks.com for anything on this page, including deletion of your account.
