What this is

Dead Talks is an archive of channelled conversations, published for reflection and contemplation. The words attributed to historical figures are received through channelling. They are offered as inspiration, not as historical record, and not as statements those people made in life.

Nothing here is medical, psychological, legal or financial advice. If you are struggling, please speak to a qualified professional.

What you write

Your reflections stay yours and stay private. Your display name, picture and "about" line are public, so keep them to something you are happy for strangers to see. Do not upload anything you do not have the right to use, or anything unlawful or abusive.

The words themselves

The sessions, recordings, artwork and audio journeys belong to their creators. You are welcome to read them, listen to them, and share a link or a quote with attribution. Please do not republish the archive wholesale or present the material as your own.
